# Labour Relations Dashboard: Project Overview

---

## 🎯 **Project Name**
**Labour Relations Dashboard**

---

## 📝 **Elevator Pitch**
The **Labour Relations Dashboard** is a **real-time, comparative platform** that empowers users to **monitor, analyze, and compare** workplace relations bodies, dispute resolution mechanisms, and compliance frameworks across **Ireland and all European countries**. It serves as a **one-stop hub** for researchers, legal professionals, HR teams, and businesses to navigate the complex landscape of labour law enforcement and workplace dispute resolution.

---

## 🌍 **Why This Project?**

### **Problem Statement**
- **Fragmented Information**: Workplace relations bodies and their functions vary widely across Europe, with no centralized resource for comparison.
- **Complex Compliance**: Businesses and employees struggle to understand **filing requirements, deadlines, and procedures** for disputes in different countries.
- **Lack of Insights**: Policymakers and researchers lack tools to **identify trends, gaps, or best practices** in labour law enforcement.

### **Solution**
A **dynamic, user-friendly dashboard** that:
1. **Aggregates** data on workplace relations bodies from **Ireland and all European countries**.
2. **Standardizes** information on **roles, responsibilities, and processes** for easy comparison.
3. **Visualizes** trends in **complaint volumes, resolution times, and enforcement actions**.
4. **Automates** report generation for **compliance, advocacy, or internal use**.

---

## 🎯 **Target Audience**
| **User Group**               | **Use Case**                                                                                     |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Researchers**               | Compare labour law enforcement across countries; identify trends for academic studies.     |
| **Policymakers**              | Benchmark national systems against EU peers; inform regulatory reforms.                     |
| **Legal Professionals**       | Quickly access **filing requirements and deadlines** for cross-border cases.                   |
| **HR Teams**                  | Ensure compliance with local labour laws; track disputes in multiple jurisdictions.           |
| **Businesses**                | Monitor **workplace relations risks** in countries of operation; optimize HR strategies.        |
| **Trade Unions/NGOs**          | Advocate for workers’ rights using **data-driven insights**.                                  |

---

## 🌟 **Key Differentiators**
✔ **First-of-its-kind**: No existing platform provides a **comparative, real-time view** of workplace relations bodies across Europe.
✔ **Open-Source**: Community-driven updates ensure **accuracy and comprehensiveness**.
✔ **Actionable Insights**: Beyond data, the dashboard offers **trend analysis, alerts, and report generation**.
✔ **Scalable**: Built to expand to **global coverage** (e.g., North America, Asia) in future phases.

---

## 📌 **Project Goals**
1. **Centralize**: Create a **single source of truth** for workplace relations data in Europe.
2. **Simplify**: Make complex labour law information **accessible and actionable**.
3. **Empower**: Enable users to **make data-driven decisions** in HR, legal, or policy contexts.
4. **Collaborate**: Foster a **community of contributors** to keep the dashboard up-to-date.

---

## 🔍 **Scope**

### **In Scope (v1.0)**
- **Geographic Coverage**: Ireland + **all 27 EU countries + UK + EEA** (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein).
- **Data Types**:
  - Workplace relations bodies (e.g., WRC, Labour Courts).
  - Dispute resolution mechanisms (mediation, adjudication, enforcement).
  - Compliance requirements (filing deadlines, procedures).
  - Historical data on **complaint volumes, resolution times, enforcement actions** (where available).
- **Features**:
  - Interactive comparison tool.
  - Compliance tracker.
  - Basic visualizations (heatmaps, trend charts).
  - Report generator (PDF/CSV).

### **Out of Scope (v1.0)**
- **Global coverage** (beyond Europe).
- **Real-time legal updates** (manual updates via community/PRs).
- **AI-powered predictions** (planned for v2.0).
- **Mobile app** (planned for v2.0).
